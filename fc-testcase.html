AOR Studio

The studio enables research for Art-therapy Oriented Reengineering (AOR) of Formative e-Assessment (FA) systems. It was founded by me as a volunteer to express my interests to research how to reengineer FA systems to serve formation/education approaches to oppression/alienation. The approaches under research are oriented by art therapy inquiries to serve oppressed or alienated adults interested in self-overcoming of their oppressions/alienations.

The studio offers research services/interventions for communities/teams of teachers, training organizers, and social-pedagogues in three groups of settings;

  1. pre-service settings
  2. in-service settings
  3. after-service settings (e.g, a setting for retired teachers)
