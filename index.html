AOR Studio

The studio enables Art-therapy Oriented Reengineering (AOR) research and therefore its name is AOR Studio. It was founded by me as a volunteer to express my research interests. When I am not software engineer, I am interested in researching education/formation practices in AOR research workshops to serve oppressed or alienated adults interested in self-overcoming of their oppressions/alienations.

The studio's first research act is to investigate how to reengineer Formative e-Assessment (FA) systems to serve integarting art-therapy formation approaches to oppression/alienation. The act is initially organized to facilitate researching how FA systems can be reengineered to integrate Joseph Beuys 's art-pedagogy approach to alination and Paulo Freire dult-literacy pedagogy approach to oppression. To put it differently, the act focuses on researching and reinventing Freire’s pedagogy of the oppressed and Beuys art-pedagogy as situated pedagogies that could be adapted for diverse places, different stakeholders and varying conditions of designers/users of FA systems. Examples of FA system designers/users can be alianted adults, oppressed adults, unemployed, social-pedagogues, formal-pedagogues (e.g., trainee-teachers) and art-pedagogues interested in understandinsg the oppressions/alienations in diverse bicultural environments.
