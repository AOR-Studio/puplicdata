AOR Studio

The studio enables Art-therapy Oriented Reengineering (AOR) research and therefore, it is referred to as AOR Studio. It was founded by me as a volunteer to express my research interests. When I am not software engineer, I am interested in researching adult-education/formation practices to serve oppressed or alienated adults interested in self-overcoming of their oppressions/alienations.
The studio's first project is about researching how to reengineer Formative e-Assessment (FA) systems for AOR tasks. The project focuses on researching and reinventing Paulo Freire’s pedagogy of the oppressed as situated pedagogy that could be adapted for diverse places, different stakeholders and varying conditions of designers/users of FA systems. Examples of FA system designers/users can be trainee-teachers, alianted adults , oppressed adults or unemployed...
