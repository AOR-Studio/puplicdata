adult oppression/alienation therapy The AOR task contexts/scopes drive and initialize the FA systems under reengineering & pedagogical investigation. Technically seen, the FA systems, are assistive technology under reengineering investigation to serve oppressed or alienated adults, and pedagogically seen, they are special tools under pedagogical investigation to support social-pedagogues, formal-pedagogues (trainee-teachers) and art-pedagogues interested in understandinsg the oppressions/alienations of the adults in diverse cultures for developing better pedagogies of liberation from oppression/alienation
